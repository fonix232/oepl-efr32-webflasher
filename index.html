OpenEPaperLink EFR32xG22 Web Flasher

Unlocks, erases and flashes EFR32xG22-based tags over SWD from your browser, replacing reflash.py.

This browser has no WebUSB (navigator.usb). Use desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera, served over HTTPS or localhost. Firefox and Safari do not support WebUSB.

VTref / 3V3VCC: either the probe supplies 3.3 V, or the tag runs on its own batteries and the probe only senses it (VTref)

Power the tag with 3.3 V or less, never 5 V. Keep wires short; the flasher uses a 1 MHz SWD clock. On Linux, add a udev rule for your probe; on Windows, the probe's CMSIS-DAP v2 / J-Link interface needs the WinUSB driver.
