OEPL Tag Flasher Source on GitHub

Reflash an EFR32 price tag from your browser

Unlock, back up and flash OpenEPaperLink EFR32xG22 tags over SWD. This page replaces reflash.py and needs no install.

This browser can’t talk to USB devices. Open the page in desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera. Firefox and Safari don’t support WebUSB.

  1. Connect your probe

    How to wire the tag
    ProbeTag
    SWCLKSWCLK
    SWDIOSWDIO
    nRESETRESET, needed after unlocking
    GNDGND
    VTref or 3V3VCC: the probe either powers the tag at 3.3 V or senses the tag’s own battery

    Use 3.3 V or less. Never connect 5 V; it destroys the chip. Keep the wires short, because the flasher runs SWD at 1 MHz. On Linux, add a udev rule for your probe. On Windows, the probe needs the WinUSB driver.
